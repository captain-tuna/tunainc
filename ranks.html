Ranks

The roles and responsibilities within Tuna Inc.

Founder

The legendary person who started it all: cat1.

Chairman

Manages the main branch and oversees the others. The Chairman is the lead developer, owns the Roblox groups, and creates other content, including in-game clothing and videos.

Vice Chairman

Manages server moderation, promotions and the Discord server. The Vice Chairman oversees applications and helps coordinate promotions.

Head Of Staff

Oversees branch leaders and members in important roles. The Head Of Staff plans events, supports members and helps keep the organisation running smoothly.

R&D Director

Develops new Plane Crazy technology concepts, lore and game ideas. The R&D Director oversees Tuna Inc.'s lore and innovation.
