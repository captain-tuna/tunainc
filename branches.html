Our Branches

The organisations that make up Tuna Inc.

Tuna Inc.

The main Tuna Inc. group, overseeing all branches. It specialises in power generation, research and technology, and owns facilities including Glaceria Research Base and Tuna Inc. Hydroelectric Dam.

Tunair

The airline of Tuna Inc. and its second-largest branch. One of the most popular branches in Plane Crazy, it operates an extensive fleet of airliners and smaller aircraft for roleplay flights and training.

Tuna Inc. Baristas Association

The owner of the Tuna Inc. Cafe franchise, with several shops around the complex. Its most infamous location sits in the middle of the lake near the old security training facility, at point C2 on the complex map.

T.U.N.A.

The Tuna Universal Navigation Agency is Tuna Inc.'s primary spaceflight organisation. Its projects include asteroid mining, space tourism supported by Tunair spaceplanes, orbital lasers, artificial light mirrors and other space-based developments.

T.M.A.

The Tuna Meteorological Agency conducts atmospheric research and helps protect against natural disasters by collecting data on weather systems. Aircraft and heavy interceptors are common sights in its fleet.

Tuna Hazards

An elite team of specialists dealing with chemicals, radiation and other harmful substances. It is the first branch to respond to spills, fires and other hazardous incidents.

Tuna Security

The largest branch of Tuna Inc. It operates TSHQ, TSTF1, TSTF2, TSTF3 and TSTF4. Its fleet numbers hundreds of aircraft and vehicles, including fighters, cargo aircraft, tanks, presidential aircraft, display aircraft, experimental designs and reconnaissance units.
